AETHERIS DYNAMICS - COMPANY FAQS
==================================================


FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Q: What is the payroll schedule?
A: Aetheris Dynamics processes payroll on a bi-weekly basis (every other Friday). Contractual employees submit invoices monthly unless their SOW states otherwise.

Q: How do performance reviews work across different roles?
A: 
- Contractual Employees: Feedback is provided at the end of the contract term or project milestone.
- Associates: Bi-annual reviews (June and December) focusing on goal achievement and skills growth.
- Managers: Bi-annual reviews, supplemented by 360-degree feedback from their direct reports and peers.
- Executives: Annual review conducted by the Board of Directors based on strategic KPIs and revenue goals.

Q: Is there a referral bonus program?
A: Yes! We encourage employees to refer top talent. Associates and Managers receive a $1,500 bonus for successful hires who stay past 90 days. Executives are excluded from the referral bonus program due to conflict of interest. Contractual employees can participate and receive a $500 bonus.

Q: Can I request to work permanently remote?
A: Aetheris operates on a hybrid model. Fully remote arrangements must be approved by your Department Head and HR. Associates generally require 2 days in-office, whereas Contractual staff may be fully remote depending on the project.

Q: Who do I contact for benefits enrollment issues?
A: Please submit a ticket via the HR Portal under the 'Benefits & Payroll' category, or email benefits@aetherisdynamics.dummy.



--- STANDARD CORPORATE CLAUSES (Part 1) ---

SECTION A: SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
This document applies to all individuals working at Aetheris Dynamics, including Contractual Employees, Associates, Managers, and Executives. The policies detailed herein are designed to foster a safe, productive, and equitable work environment. All personnel are expected to read, understand, and adhere to the guidelines set forth. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.

The scope of this policy extends to all physical office locations, remote work environments, company-sponsored events, and any external interactions where an individual is representing Aetheris Dynamics. The management reserves the right to amend, modify, or revoke any part of this policy at its sole discretion, with or without prior notice, subject to applicable local, state, and federal laws.

SECTION B: DEFINITIONS OF EMPLOYEE CATEGORIES
1. Contractual Employees: Individuals hired for a specific duration or project. They are generally exempt from standard benefits unless otherwise specified in their contract.
2. Associates: Full-time or part-time entry-to-mid level employees involved in day-to-day operational, technical, or administrative tasks.
3. Managers: Employees responsible for supervising teams, managing departmental budgets, and executing strategic directives.
4. Executives: C-suite leaders, Vice Presidents, and Senior Directors who guide the overall strategic vision and corporate governance of Aetheris Dynamics.

SECTION C: COMPLIANCE, VIOLATIONS, AND DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
Strict compliance with this policy is mandatory. Aetheris Dynamics employs a zero-tolerance approach towards intentional violations of core company guidelines. Any suspected violation should be immediately reported to the Human Resources Department, the Compliance Officer, or via the anonymous corporate whistleblower hotline.

Investigations into alleged violations will be conducted promptly, impartially, and confidentially. Disciplinary actions may include verbal warnings, written reprimands, performance improvement plans (PIPs), suspension without pay, or immediate termination. Contractual employees may face immediate termination of their contract without severance.

SECTION D: POLICY ADMINISTRATION AND REVIEW
The Human Resources and Legal Departments are jointly responsible for the administration, interpretation, and application of this policy. This policy will be reviewed on an annual basis to ensure alignment with evolving legal standards, industry best practices, and organizational goals.
Any exceptions to this policy must be approved in writing by a C-level Executive and documented in the employee's personnel file. Employees who have questions regarding the interpretation of any clause within this document should proactively reach out to their HR Business Partner for clarification.
